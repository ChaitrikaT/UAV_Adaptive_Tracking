| 7->8 | 0.4671 [0.369, 0.5543] | 0.418 [0.3345, 0.5074] | 0.9933 | INCONCLUSIVE / INCONCLUSIVE |
| 8->7 | 0.438 [0.3351, 0.5559] | 0.4705 [0.3973, 0.5461] | 0.9844 | INCONCLUSIVE / NOT SUPPORTED |

- Both held-out AUCs are below 0.5 with intervals that include 0.5: **the difficulty estimator does not predict difficulty on an unseen clip** (it fits its training clip: blocked-CV AUC 0.82/0.77, then fails to transfer).
- 4 features change the SIGN of their standardised coefficient between the two training directions: the learned relationship is clip-specific.
- The difficulty policy chose ROBUST for >98% of frames on both validation clips: it did not adapt, it collapsed to always-ROBUST (most frames are 'hard' for FAST, so the estimator almost never says 'easy').

- Detector model-call time on this GPU: FAST/ROBUST = [('34.152', '33.966'), ('37.138', '37.21')]: **ROBUST costs essentially the same as FAST**, so there is no compute to save by adapting. End-to-end cost is ~45.342 ms/frame for either mode (decode and tracking dominate).
- Conclusion for Objective 3: the adaptive mechanism is implemented and ran online, but on this detector and GPU it gives **no measurable quality-cost benefit over always-ROBUST** (verdicts INCONCLUSIVE / NOT SUPPORTED).

## 8. What can and cannot be claimed

**Can:** a complete offline pipeline (detector, own-ID tracker, difficulty estimator, FAST/ROBUST switching, metrics) exists and was run on real clips; ROBUST detects more than FAST on validation clips; replay equals online; all identity metrics were measured and are low; the difficulty estimator and adaptive policy were tested honestly and gave negative/inconclusive results; the system fails on the reserved clips.
**Cannot:** that tracking is accurate, that identities are consistent, that conditions predict difficulty, that adaptation improves quality or saves compute, that results generalise to clips 9-10, or anything about edge hardware.

## 9. Limitations

Single dataset; detector trained 12 epochs; clips 7-8 informed detector selection; frames without reference rows are not scored (project variant of the protocol, not TrackEval); tracker thresholds tuned on a coarse 3x3 grid at its edge; cost figures are from one laptop GPU; no CPU or edge timing; occlusion not modelled.
